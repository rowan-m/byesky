Clean up who you follow on Bluesky

Nimbye helps you review the accounts you follow on Bluesky and spot inactive, muted, or one-way profiles. Set your own scoring weights, see who interacts with you, lock favourites you want to keep, and unfollow in bulk or one by one. Sign in with your Bluesky handle to get started.

What Nimbye asks permission for

  • Read who you follow, your notifications, and public profiles and posts, to score each account.
  • Follow and unfollow accounts, only when you confirm it. Nimbye can't post, like, block or change your profile.
  • List your DM conversations, to see who you talk to. Nimbye never sends messages.

Bluesky's sign-in screen uses broader labels for these, such as "Access specific parts of your Bluesky account" and "Chat: Read and send messages". Everything Nimbye finds stays in this browser.

Analyzing the accounts you follow

Nimbye works at a gentle pace so your other Bluesky apps keep working while it runs. Large follow lists can take 10 minutes or more, and you can keep this tab in the background.
