Clean up your Bluesky Followings

ByeSky helps you identify inactive, muted, or low-value accounts in your followings. Set your own scoring rules, see who doesn't follow back, and safely clean up your list in seconds. Sign in with your Bluesky account to get started.

What ByeSky asks permission for

  • Read who you follow, your notifications, and public profiles and posts, to score each account.
  • Follow and unfollow accounts, only when you confirm it. ByeSky can't post, like, block or change your profile.
  • List your DM conversations, to see who you talk to. ByeSky never sends messages.

Bluesky's sign-in screen uses broader labels for these, such as "Access specific parts of your Bluesky account" and "Chat: Read and send messages". Everything ByeSky finds stays in this browser.

Analyzing Followings

ByeSky works at a gentle pace so your other Bluesky apps keep working while it runs. Large follow lists can take 10 minutes or more, and you can keep this tab in the background.
